### For your literature review, the most relevant ones to study in depth because our main focus is on SMEs and no-code are:

1. Zapier – market leader, trigger-action model, largest app library
2. Make – visual scenario-builder, more powerful branching/logic than Zapier
3. n8n – open-source, self-hostable, node-based
4. IFTTT – simplest/most consumer-oriented model, good for showing the "too simple" end of the spectrum
5. Workato / MuleSoft / Boomi – good examples of the "enterprise-grade, too expensive/complex for SMEs" side of your problem statement
6. Zoho Flow / Pabbly Connect – closer competitors in the SME-affordable space, worth comparing against directly
