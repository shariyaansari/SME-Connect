Drawbacks
Users have reported slow performance or lag, particularly when handling high volumes of data, along with a learning curve because the platform has many features and configurations. Some reviews also mention that reports and dashboards can be difficult to interpret or may not always provide sufficiently actionable insights, meaning managers may still need to analyse data manually. There are also reported concerns around integration issues, occasional technical glitches, notification delays, and support responsiveness.
