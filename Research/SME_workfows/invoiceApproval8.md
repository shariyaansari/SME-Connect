The actual time and pain come from the delay and the risk:

Three-Way Matching (The Real Pain): The most critical step is matching the Invoice to the Purchase Order (PO) and the Receiving Report. If any of those three documents don't align perfectly (wrong quantity, incorrect vendor on the PO), the human has to step in, manually investigate, and get approval. This is where invoices get lost or delayed.

Wrong Approvals & Duplicates: The actual risk is paying the wrong amount or paying twice. This requires a human to manually verify the required approval chain based on the dollar amount or department, and then manually check the accounting system for duplicates before final entry.
