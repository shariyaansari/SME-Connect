Business type: Local Property Management SME
Process: Accounts Payable & Supplier Invoice Approvals
Tool 1: Email (Gmail / Outlook)
Tool 2: Cloud Storage (Google Drive / OneDrive)
Tool 3: Financial Tracking Database (Airtable / Google Sheets)
Tool 4: Accounting Software (QuickBooks Online / Xero)
Trigger: Vendor or contractor emails an invoice PDF to the main inbox
Manual step: Admin staff opens the email, downloads the invoice, and manually reads the line items
Action: Type invoice details (amount, vendor name, invoice number, due date) into the tracking sheet and forward the email to the manager for a signature
Frequency: 15–20 invoices per day
Problem: Human data-entry typos (e.g., misreading decimal points), high risk of double-paying duplicate invoices, and approval bottlenecks when managers travel
Current workaround: The bookkeeper physically prints out out standing invoices and leaves them on the owner's desk every Friday afternoon for signed confirmation
Automation opportunity: High. Invoices can be auto-forwarded to a parser tool (like Parseur or Make AI Extractors) to extract data, instantly run a duplicate check against the database, and ping the manager via Slack or an interactive email button for a 1-click digital approval. Once approved, the record pushes seamlessly into QuickBooks.
